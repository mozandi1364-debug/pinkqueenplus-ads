Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

PinkQueenPlus Ads respects the privacy of people who contact us about advertising and partnership opportunities. This policy explains the information that may be handled when you use this website or contact us.

Information you provide

You may provide a name, business or brand name, contact details, email address, website, social media links, campaign information, messages, and files or images if an external form offers an upload option. Please provide only information relevant to your inquiry.

How information is used

Information is used to review advertising proposals, assess business and audience fit, respond to inquiries, communicate with you, and discuss possible collaborations.

Forms and third-party services

Our campaign submission form is provided by Tally. Information and files submitted through that form are processed by Tally and may be made available to us for handling your inquiry. Tally's own privacy policy and terms apply to its processing and storage.

If you contact us through Telegram, that service processes information according to its own terms and privacy policy. Links to external websites may also take you to services with separate privacy practices.

Embedded videos and external content

This website embeds or links to content hosted by third parties, including YouTube and iSpot. When an embedded player loads, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies. You can avoid loading an embedded player by not interacting with it and can use the external watch links instead.

Sharing and publication

We do not intentionally sell inquiry information or publish private contact details. Information may be shared when necessary to handle your request, when you ask us to share it, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion requests

We aim to retain information we receive only for as long as reasonably needed to handle inquiries and maintain relevant business records. Third-party providers may retain information under their own policies. To ask us to correct or delete information held by us, contact @mozandi64 on Telegram. Requests concerning information held by a third-party provider may need to be directed to that provider.

Security

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries. However, no website, messaging service or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and sensitive information

This website is intended for businesses and people submitting advertising inquiries. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, banking credentials, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when our website, services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
