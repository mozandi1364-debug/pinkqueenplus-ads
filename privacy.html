Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

PinkQueenPlus Ads respects the privacy of people who contact us about advertising and partnership opportunities.

Information we may receive

When you submit an inquiry, you may provide your name, business or brand name, email address, website, social media links, campaign details and other information you choose to include.

How information is used

We use submitted information to review advertising proposals, assess audience and business fit, respond to inquiries and discuss possible collaborations.

Third-party services

Our submission form is provided by Tally. Information entered into that form is processed by Tally under its own privacy and service terms. If you contact us through Telegram or another external service, that service's privacy terms also apply.

Sharing and publication

We do not intentionally publish private contact details or sell inquiry information. Information may be shared only when needed to operate the inquiry process, comply with applicable law, or when you ask us to share it.

Retention and requests

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for communication, review and record-keeping. To ask a question or request correction or deletion of information we hold, contact us through @mozandi64 on Telegram.

Information you should not submit

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, banking credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive personal information.

Security and changes

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries, but no website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may be updated as our services change.
